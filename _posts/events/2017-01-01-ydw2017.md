September 29 – October 1, 2017 at Kinhaven Music School in Weston, VT.

## Talent

### Maia McCormick — Calling contras

Coming in from New York City, Maia calls and choreographs contras with capital consequences. Previously, she ran the Williams College dance and she serves on CDNY's Outreach and Retention Committee.

### Dan Blim — Calling English

Punster, musicologist, and master of charm Dan Blim sweeps the floor and wipes the walls (figuratively) with all manner of English choreographic beasts.

### Sassafras Stomp — Playing for contras and more

Johanna Davis, Adam Nordell, and John Pranio. Uplifting and soulful music from Maine and Montana.

### The Ripples — Playing for English and more

Gabrielle Lanza, Kristen Planeaux, Andrew Taylor, and Christopher Wood. The Ripples pair musical variety with a feel-good attitude.

### DJ Beats and Tea — Friday night techno contra

Baltimore's DJ Beats and Tea wields a custom-programmed DJ setup built around contra dancing, with smooth live mixing of house, trance, techno, and pop music.
