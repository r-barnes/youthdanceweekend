September 2025 at Hulbert Outdoor Center in Fairlee, VT. The first year at a new venue — Hulbert — after years at Kinhaven Music School in Weston, VT.

## Schedule

### FRIDAY

* 4:00–6:00 PM: Arrival & Check-In *(Porch of main building)*
  * Please be prepared to show a negative rapid test to a Committee member after arrival on site
  * Second test 24 hours later before Saturday dinner
* 6:30–7:30 PM: Dinner *(Dining Hall)* — Potluck
* 8:00–11:30 PM: Welcome Dance *(Dance Hall)* — Contra, announcements, then English
* 11:30 PM: Snack! *(Dining Hall)*
* Midnight onward: Line dancing *(Upper Dining Hall)*

### SATURDAY

* 8:30–9:30 AM: Breakfast *(Dining Hall)*
* 9:30–10:45 AM: Balfolk *(Dance Hall)* — Helen, Rose, and Guillaume
* 11:00 AM–12:15 PM: English Country Dancing *(Dance Hall)*
* 12:30–1:30 PM: Lunch and Rapid Testing *(outside Dining Hall / Dining Hall)*
  * Please present your negative test to a Committee member to be checked off the list.
* 1:45–2:45 PM: Camper-led Activities
  * Line Dancing 2.0 *(Dance Hall)*
  * Sea Shanty Sing *(Pirate Ship!!!)*
* 3:00–4:00 PM: Contra Lab *(Dance Hall)*
* 4:45–5:45 PM: Community Conversation
* 6:00–7:00 PM: Dinner *(Dining Hall)*
* 7:30–11:45 PM: Evening Dance *(Dance Hall)* — Contra & English, with Charts and Graphs at the break!
* 11:30 PM: Snack! *(Dining Hall)*
* Midnight and straight on 'til morning (or not):
  * Fusion Dancing *(Upper Dining Hall)*
  * Jamming *(Party Barn)*
  * Pub Sing *(The Cave)*

### SUNDAY

* 8:00–9:00 AM: Breakfast *(Dining Hall)*
* 9:00–9:30 AM: Cabin Cleanout
* 9:30–10:30 AM: Morning Waltzing *(Dance Hall)* — Pegasus Collective
* 10:30 AM–Noon: Camper-led Contra *(Dance Hall)*
* Noon–12:45 PM: Lunch *(Dining Hall)*
* 12:45 PM: Photos!
* 1:15–3:00 PM: Farewell Dance *(Dance Hall)*
* 3:00 PM: Farewell Snacks! *(Front Porch)*
* After 3:00 PM: Cleaning, goodbyes, help moving furniture!
  * Pack your bags, and sweep out your cabin. Help us pack up Hobart.
  * We love you, but you absolutely gotta leave by 4 PM. See you next year!

## Notes

* 2025 marked the move from Kinhaven Music School in Weston, VT to **Hulbert Outdoor Center in Fairlee, VT**.
* The Pirate Ship and The Cave are venue features unique to Hulbert.
