## Talent

### [Helen & Rose](https://www.rose-jackson.com/) — Contra band

Helen Kuhar (guitar, vocals, harmonium) and Rose Jackson (fiddle, vocals). Drawing on traditional tunes from Ireland, New England, Quebec, and France, Helen & Rose craft vocal harmonies for age-old songs with modern relevance. Rose Jackson grew up in the folk scene of western Massachusetts and has toured contra dance festivals with bands including Polaris and Stove Dragon; Helen Kuhar hails from Cambridge, MA and plays for contra dances around the country.
