September 14–16, 2018 at Kinhaven Music School, Weston, VT. The 9th annual Youth Dance Weekend.

## Talent

### [Dana Parkinson](https://www.danadancecaller.com/) — Caller

### [Louise Siddons](https://scissortail.org/who-we-are/callers/louise-siddons/) — Caller

### [Nova](https://nova-trio.com/) — Contra band

Everest Whitman, Guillaume Sparrow-Pepin, and Kathleen Fownes.

### The Jig Lords — English band

Kendall Rogers, Marnen Laibow-Koser, and Brad Battey.

### [DJ Beats and Tea](https://www.jaredkirkpatrickmusic.com/) — Friday night techno contra

Baltimore's DJ Beats and Tea wields a custom-programmed DJ setup built around contra dancing, with smooth live mixing of house, trance, techno, and pop music.
