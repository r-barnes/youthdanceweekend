Youth Dance Weekend 2020 and 2021 were cancelled due to the COVID-19 pandemic. This was the only interruption in YDW's annual run since its founding in 2008.

## 2020

The committee had spent the fall and winter of 2019–2020 planning for YDW 2020 and welcomed a new committee member before the pandemic began. In a message to the community, they wrote:

> *"We are cancelling Youth Dance Weekend 2020. The coronavirus pandemic is still rampant in the United States and there is no safe way to gather people for an event."*

The committee considered holding a virtual event but ultimately decided against it given the difficulty of organizing remotely during the crisis.

## 2021

YDW also did not take place in 2021 as the pandemic continued. The committee reformed with new members and began planning for a return in 2022.

**2020 committee:** Coral Breuer, Dana Parkinson (new — she had called at YDW 2018), Donal Sheets, Maria Cramer, Max Newman, and Robin Banerji. Harris Lapiroff and Marisa Keller stepped down.

## Return in 2022

YDW returned in September 2022 with a brand new committee and new COVID precautions (including rapid testing on arrival and before Saturday evening). See [YDW 2022](/events/ydw2022/) for the full schedule.
