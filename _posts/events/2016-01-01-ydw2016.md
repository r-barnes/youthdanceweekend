September 23–25, 2016 at Kinhaven Music School, Weston, VT. The 7th annual Youth Dance Weekend, and the 6th year at Kinhaven.

## Talent

### Michael Karcher — Calling contras

![Michael Karcher](https://web.archive.org/web/20160926182836im_/http://youthdanceweekend.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Michael-Karcher-150x150.jpg)

Michael Karcher calls captivating contras. His name is increasingly popping up at dance events all over the U.S. and Canada. He is a lovely fellow, both on mic and in casual conversation.

### Miriam Newman — Calling English

![Miriam Newman](https://web.archive.org/web/20160926182836im_/http://youthdanceweekend.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Miriam-Newman-150x150.jpg)

Miriam Newman called for YDW several years ago and returned for this year. She started calling in 2006 at the Swarthmore College Folkdance Club, taught by Joanna Reiner. After that she moved to New York City and participated in Country Dance*New York's ECD caller's apprenticeship program. She has choreographed a number of lovely English dances.

### Pete's Posse — Playing for contras, English, and more

![Pete's Posse](https://web.archive.org/web/20160926182836im_/http://youthdanceweekend.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/PosseCTrio1-300x158.jpg)

Pete's Posse is composed of acclaimed musician Pete Sutherland (fiddle, piano, banjo and vocals), his protégé Oliver Scanlon (fiddle, mandolin, dobro, foot percussion and vocals), and the dynamic Tristan Henderson (guitars, mandolin, jaw harp, bodhran, foot percussion and vocals). Vermont's multigenerational energy-machine.

### Turnip the Beet — Playing for contras and more

![Turnip the Beet](https://web.archive.org/web/20160926182836im_/http://youthdanceweekend.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Turnipthebeet-300x200.jpg)

Turnip the Beet is a dance band duo from Nashville: Kelsey Wells and Jeremy Lekich. A much-requested YDW addition.

### Aaron Marcus — Additional musician

### Rushfest — Friday night techno contra

Emily Rush presents her late-night phenomenon, Rushfest: a late-night alternative music contra dance party.
