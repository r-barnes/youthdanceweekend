September 23–25, 2016 at Kinhaven Music School in Weston, VT (our 5th year at Kinhaven).

## Talent

### Michael Karcher — Calling contras

Michael Karcher calls captivating contras. His name is increasingly popping up at dance events all over the U.S. and Canada.

### Miriam Newman — Calling English

Miriam Newman called for YDW several years ago. She started calling in 2006 at the Swarthmore College Folkdance Club. After that she moved to New York City and participated in Country Dance\*New York's ECD caller's apprenticeship program. She has also choreographed a number of lovely English dances.

### Pete's Posse — Playing for contras, English, and more

Pete's Posse is composed of Pete Sutherland (fiddle, piano, banjo and vocals), Oliver Scanlon (fiddle, mandolin, dobro, foot percussion and vocals), and Tristan Henderson (guitars, mandolin, jaw harp, bodhran, foot percussion and vocals). Vermont's multigenerational energy-machine.

### Turnip the Beet — Playing for contras and more

Turnip the Beet is a dance band duo from Nashville: Kelsey Wells and Jeremy Lekich.

### Aaron Marcus — Additional musician

### Rushfest — Friday night techno contra

Emily Rush presents her late-night phenomenon, Rushfest: a late-night alternative music contra dance party.
